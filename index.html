These positions use Elite Dangerous's orbital-element convention (ascending node and argument of periapsis are mirrored from the standard astronomical one), matching the Canonn signals page. Positions of bodies orbiting a barycentre, like 2 h and 2 i, have not been verified against observations, so treat margins of a few thousand kilometres as uncertain.
